# Know Who I'm

<img src="/images/about.svg" alt="About illustration" class="about-image" />

Hi everyone! I'm **Olivier Page**, a **Full-stack Developer** based in
**Thionville, France**.

I specialize in **Java/.NET**, **Angular**, and **Azure**, with over a decade of
experience building robust, scalable software for banking, energy, and asset
management.

I hold a **Master 2 in Computer Science** from **Université Grenoble Alpes**.
